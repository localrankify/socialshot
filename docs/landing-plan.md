# SocialShot landing page: section plan

The competitor (a desktop video editor) sells "edit faster on your PC". SocialShot sells something they can't:
**clips made while the stream is still live, with nothing to install.** Every section below leans on that.
Their page is used only for rhythm and layout ideas (split feature rows, a proof strip, a time-saved
calculator, a comparison table, a big wordmark footer). No copy, names or visuals are taken from it.

Look: keep the current dark sheet, grey type, one purple for the main action. Product pieces drawn in
real UI (like the hero demo), not stock icons. No gradients, glows, sparkles or emoji.

---

## 1. Hero (done)
- Badge: `Live` tag + "Clips Twitch, Kick and YouTube streams as they happen"
- H1: "Turn long streams into short clips, / while you're still live."
- Lede, two buttons (Start for free / See how it works), three checkmarks
- Animated dashboard demo, then the "Clips from Twitch · Kick · YouTube · Your own files" row

## 2. Numbers strip (new)
One quiet row of 4 facts, label above number, no icons:
- `~2 min` to clip a 15-minute video
- `3` free clips on every video
- `9:16` vertical, captioned, ready to post (replaced an unbacked "5–10 clips per hour" figure)
- `0` things to install

## 3. Feature row: Live clipping (new, text left, picture right)
- Eyebrow: Live clipping
- H2: "Your first clip is ready before the stream ends."
- Body: Add a creator once. SocialShot watches the stream, spots when chat and voice spike, and hands you the clip a few minutes later.
- 3 bullets: Follows creators automatically · Clips while they're live · Works on Twitch, Kick and YouTube Live
- Picture: the existing chat-activity wave with "Clip saved" markers, larger

## 4. Feature row: The AI picks the moments (new, picture left)
- H2: "It finds the part people actually react to."
- Body: Every moment gets a score from what was said, how chat moved and how the voice changed. You see why it picked it.
- Picture: the scored rows (94 / 88 / 81) with a one-line reason under each

## 5. Feature row: Captions + caption styles (merge current "Caption styles")
- H2: "Word-by-word captions, already on."
- Body: Captions are written, timed and styled for you. Pick a look, or change font, colour and size in the editor.
- Picture: phone clip with words lighting up, then the horizontal row of caption style cards under it

## 6. Feature row: Editor (new, picture left)
- H2: "Fix anything in a few clicks."
- Body: Trim the start, fix a word, swap the layout to face-cam on top. Save, and it renders again.
- Picture: the timeline with trim handles

## 7. Feature row: Posting (new, text left)
- H2: "From stream to Shorts without downloading a file."
- Body: Connect YouTube and Instagram, queue clips, and they post on your schedule.
- Picture: the posting queue (YouTube / Instagram / TikTok rows)

## 8. Bento: "Everything between the stream and the post" (current features grid, trimmed)
Small cards for what didn't get a full row: vertical 9:16 auto-framing, titles and hooks written for you,
batch download as ZIP, works in any browser, team-friendly (one account, many creators), your own uploads.

## 9. How it works (current, keep)
1. Paste a link or add a creator · 2. SocialShot finds the moments · 3. Edit, download or post

## 10. Time saved + comparison (new)
