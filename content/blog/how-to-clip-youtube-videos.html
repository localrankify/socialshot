"How to clip YouTube videos" gets thousands of searches a month, and in 2026 the answer changed. In April, YouTube announced it was replacing the viewer Clip button with Share at timestamp. If the scissors icon has gone from your account, that is why. Here is what still works for sharing a moment, and how to clip your own videos and streams into Shorts.

What happened to YouTube Clips

Clips let viewers pick 5 to 60 seconds of a video or stream and share it as a link. On 16 April 2026 YouTube said it would stop viewers from making new Clips and push Share at timestamp on phones instead. Clips made before the change still play. The rollout is gradual, so you may still see the old button on one account and not on another. YouTube also said clipping tools for creators are coming to Shorts.

Share a moment from any video

  1. On a phone: pause at the moment, tap Share, turn on the start-at-time option, then copy or send the link.
  2. On a computer: pause, right-click the video and choose Copy video URL at current time.
  3. By hand: add ?t=95 to the end of a youtu.be link to start at 95 seconds.

This shares a starting point, not a cut clip. The viewer watches the full video from that second.

Clip your own YouTube videos into Shorts

If the video is yours, you can cut real clips and post them as Shorts.

  • In the YouTube app. Open your video, choose the option to make a Short from it, and pick up to 60 seconds. It is quick, but you choose the moment by hand and captions are basic.
  • In a video editor. Download your video from YouTube Studio, then cut, crop to 9:16 and caption it yourself. Full control, but slow when you have hours of footage.
  • With a clip maker. Paste the video link. A YouTube Shorts maker finds the strongest moments, crops them vertical and adds captions, so you review clips instead of scrubbing a timeline.

Clip a YouTube live stream

Streams are where clipping by hand hurts most: a three-hour stream might have five moments worth posting. With a live stream clipper you add your channel once and moments are saved while you are still live, ready to post that night. The same works for long podcast episodes.

Clipping someone else's video is different from sharing a timestamp. Downloading and reposting another creator's content needs their permission, unless your use is clearly covered by fair use where you live. Paid clipping campaigns give you that permission in writing.

Make the clip work as a Short

  • Start on the moment. The first second decides whether people keep watching.
  • Keep it tight: most good Shorts are 20 to 45 seconds.
  • Add word-by-word captions; many people watch without sound.
  • Write a title that says what happens, not "crazy moment".
