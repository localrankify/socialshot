Cricket is one of the biggest short-form niches in India. Searches for cricket Shorts and cricket Reels run into thousands every month, and every big series brings a fresh wave. The catch is copyright: the fastest way to lose a cricket channel is to repost match footage. This guide covers what you can clip, what works, and how to post during a series.

First: you cannot repost the broadcast

Live match footage and official highlights belong to the broadcaster and the cricket board. Clipping the TV feed or official highlights and posting them as Shorts usually gets a copyright claim, a muted video or a strike. Even a few seconds can be matched automatically.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. When in doubt, use footage you created or have written permission to use.

Cricket content you can clip

  • Watch-along streams. You and your chat reacting live, with the score on screen as text, not the broadcast. Your reactions are the content.
  • Reaction and opinion videos. Your take on a declaration, a dropped catch or a selection, filmed on your camera.
  • Cricket podcasts. Two or three people arguing about the XI make great clips. See how to make podcast clips.
  • Analysis with your own visuals. Field placements drawn on a whiteboard or app, stats you made into a graphic.
  • Grassroots cricket. Club matches, gully cricket and net sessions you filmed yourself, with the players happy to be posted.

Formats that work

The instant reaction

The moment a wicket falls in your watch-along, your face and your chat are the clip. Post it within the hour while people are searching for that match.

The hot take

One clear opinion in under 40 seconds: "This is why the second new ball decided the Test." Put the claim on screen as the hook.

The explainer

Short and visual: why a fielder was moved, what a spell did to the run rate. These keep getting views after the match.

Captions that fit your audience

Much cricket talk in India happens in Hinglish. Captions in Roman letters are easier for most viewers to read on a phone than Devanagari or Urdu script. SocialShot can write Hindi and Urdu speech in Roman letters and time it word by word. See auto captions.

Riding a big series

Search interest jumps around every big match. As of mid-November 2026, India's tour of New Zealand is reported to finish with two World Test Championship Tests, starting in Wellington on 19 November and moving to Christchurch from 27 November. Fixtures can change, so check the official BCCI and New Zealand Cricket schedules.

  • Before each day: a short preview or prediction clip.
  • During play: go live with a watch-along and let a live stream clipper save your reactions as they happen.
  • After stumps: two or three of your best reactions and one clear opinion clip.

Use the match in your titles the way people search for it, for example "India vs New Zealand 1st Test: why that spell changed the game". Keep thumbnails and titles honest about what is in the clip.

Quick checklist

  • No broadcast or official highlight footage.
  • Your face, your voice or your own camera in every clip.
  • A hook in the first second and captions on every word.
  • Posted while the match is still being talked about.
