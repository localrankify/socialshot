Long video in, Shorts out

Cutting Shorts by hand means watching the whole video again, finding a moment, reframing it, captioning every word and exporting it, once per Short. SocialShot does that part. You choose which clips to keep.

  • Works from YouTube, Twitch and Kick links, or your own files
  • Vertical 9:16 at 1080×1920, with the speaker kept in frame
  • A hook on screen and a title ready to paste

What makes a Short worth watching

A moment, not a minute

Each clip starts close to the payoff. SocialShot looks for a line, a reaction or a turn people respond to.

Readable with the sound off

Most Shorts are watched muted at first. Word-by-word captions keep people watching until they tap for sound.

Posted often

One long video can become several Shorts. Put them on a schedule so your channel posts every day.
