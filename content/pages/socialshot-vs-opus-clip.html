Where Opus Clip is strong

Opus Clip is a mature tool with a large user base, good clip selection on finished videos and plenty of templates. If you mostly clip uploaded long-form videos and post every week, a subscription can make sense.

Why streamers pick SocialShot

  • It clips while you are still live, so you post before the stream ends
  • No monthly fee: quiet weeks cost nothing, busy weeks cost per clip
  • Kick, Twitch and YouTube channels followed in one account
  • A score and a reason on every clip, so you know why it was picked

Pricing, in plain numbers

SocialShot

3 free clips on every video, with no card. After that, 1 credit makes 1 more clip. No subscription, so you never pay for a month you did not clip.

Opus Clip

A free plan with a monthly allowance and a watermark, then subscriptions reported at $15 a month (Starter) and $29 a month (Pro). Credits on monthly plans are reported to expire.

Which costs less?

If you clip a few videos a month, pay-per-clip usually costs less. If you clip every day at high volume, compare a full month of each with your own numbers.
