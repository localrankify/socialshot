FAQ

Questions people ask first

Everything people ask before their first clip.

Start for free
01Is SocialShot free?

Yes, to start. Every video gives you 3 clips for free. When you want more clips from a video, buy credits: 1 credit makes 1 more clip. There is no subscription.

02Do I need to install anything?

No. Sign up, paste a link and SocialShot does the rest in your browser. There is nothing to download and no AI key to set up.

03Does it work while I'm streaming?

Yes. Add the creator once and SocialShot records whenever they go live, saving clips during the stream so the first ones are ready before it ends.

04Who owns the clips?

The clips you make are yours to download and post. The original stream still belongs to its creator, so follow their rules for clipping and posting.

05How fast are the clips?

A 15-minute video usually comes back in about two minutes. Live streams hand over their first clips while the stream is still going.

06Which videos work best?

Anything with people talking and reacting: live streams, gaming VODs, podcasts and interviews, from YouTube, Twitch, Kick or your own files.

07Can I change a clip afterwards?

Yes. Open any clip in the editor to fix captions, change the font and colours, trim it, and save it again.

08Can I clip any streamer?

SocialShot is a tool. You are responsible for having the right to clip and post a creator's content; most clipping programs ask you to follow the creator's own rules.
