SocialShot for Windows

SocialShot now runs on your own PC, free, with your own AI key. Everything below is in this release: how videos come in, how moments are found, the clips, the editor, your brand, and posting.

  • Free Windows app
  • Live stream clipping
  • 38 caption styles
  • One-click AI editor
  • Auto-post and schedule

Windows app5

NewFree Windows appThe whole clipping engine on your own PC, for Windows 10 and 11. Every feature, no subscription.
NewSetup that checks your PCSign in, it checks your computer and installs what is missing, then sits in the tray while it works.
NewBring your own AI keyOpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Groq, OpenRouter, or Ollama on your own machine. You pay your provider only for what you use.
NewWorks without a keyNo key yet? Clips are still picked from the loudest and busiest moments of the stream.
NewSign in once from the appA one-time code links the Windows app to your account in your browser, Google sign-in included.

Getting videos in5

Paste a linkA YouTube, Twitch or Kick link is all it takes to start.
Upload big filesDrop in a recording from your computer. Large files go up in pieces, so a dropped connection does not start over.
Record live streamsClips while the stream is still running, or tap to clip the best moment right now.
Creators that record themselvesAdd a creator once: recording starts when they go live, clips when someone says a word you pick, and a limit on clips per hour.
Members-only streamsAdd your browser cookies and streams you can watch can be recorded too.

Finding the moments3

Scored from words, voice and chatEvery moment is scored from what was said, how loud it got and how fast chat moved, and each clip shows why it was picked.
More clips on requestLiked the first batch? Ask for more from the same video.
Hooks, titles and hashtagsEach clip gets a hook on screen, a title, a description and hashtags, written from what was said.

Clips7

Four shapes9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, plus 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9.
Nine layoutsSplit with the face cam below, picture in picture, trio, face grid, pop-out, centre crop, zoomed in, full frame and post card.
Face cam found for youThe streamer's camera is found automatically and kept in frame.
38 caption stylesWord-by-word captions in looks like Editorial, Karaoke, Bounce, Hype, Neon, Glass box and Podcast.
14 fontsPoppins, Mazzard, Anton, Bebas Neue, Montserrat, Bangers and more for captions and hooks.
Hindi and Urdu in Roman lettersHinglish captions written the way people actually type them.
Re-listenWords came out wrong? Re-listen to a clip and get fresh captions.

Editor6

One-click AI editorPlans music, zooms and sound effects for the whole clip in one go.
Fix captions word by wordEdit any line, and click a word to jump straight to it in the clip.
Trim and clean upMulti-shot trimming, and filler words and long pauses removed for you.
Zooms, looks, intro and outroZoom into the big moments, pick a colour look, add an intro and an outro.
Music and sound effectsBuilt-in music and sound effects, with the voice level kept clear.
Made for speedUndo and redo, safe zones for each app, templates and keyboard shortcuts.

Your brand2

Brand kitYour logo, saved caption styles and a watermark with your social handles.
Your music and mediaKeep your own music, pictures and outros in one library.

Posting4

YouTube, Instagram and TikTokConnect each account once and post from SocialShot.
Auto-post with your rulesPost only clips above a score, with a gap between posts and a daily limit.
ScheduleA calendar and a board of scheduled, posted and failed clips. Drag to plan, in any time zone.
Download one or allDownload a single clip, or every clip from a video in one ZIP.

Account3

Alerts on Discord and TelegramGet told when clips are ready, with a test button to check it works.
See what AI costsAn AI usage page shows the tokens behind every video.
Your account, your wayGoogle sign-in, email check, password reset, light or dark look, and deleting your account any time.
