Common questions

Not sure it fits your stream? Paste one link and see what gets picked.

01Does it work for video podcasts?

Yes. Video podcasts work best: SocialShot keeps the speaker in frame when it cuts to vertical. Upload the episode file or paste a YouTube link.

02Can it handle two or more hosts?

Yes. Clips are chosen from what was said, so conversations between several people work well. Use the editor to change the layout if you want both faces on screen.

03How many clips do I get from one episode?

It depends on the episode: SocialShot keeps every moment worth posting. The Windows app is free; you only pay your AI provider for your own key.

04Are the captions accurate?

Speech is transcribed on our servers and timed to each word. You can fix any word in the editor before you post.
