01Can SocialShot clip my Twitch stream while I am live?

Yes. Add your channel once and SocialShot starts recording when you go live. It saves a clip each time chat and your voice spike, so the first clips are ready while the stream is still running.

02Can I clip an old Twitch VOD?

Yes. Paste the VOD link on the home screen and press Make clips. A 15-minute video usually comes back in about two minutes; longer VODs take longer.

03Is this different from Twitch's own clip button?

Twitch's clip button saves a short horizontal piece of a stream that someone chose by hand. SocialShot picks the moments for you, cuts them to vertical 9:16, adds captions and a hook, and can post them to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

04Can I clip other streamers?

SocialShot is a tool. You need the right to clip and post someone else's stream, and most clipping programs ask you to follow the creator's own rules.
