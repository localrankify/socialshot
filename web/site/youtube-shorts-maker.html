Long video in, Shorts out

What makes a Short worth watching

A moment, not a minute

Each clip starts close to the payoff. SocialShot looks for a line, a reaction or a turn people respond to.

Readable with the sound off

Most Shorts are watched muted at first. Word-by-word captions keep people watching until they tap for sound.

Posted often

One long video can become several Shorts. Put them on a schedule so your channel posts every day.

01How do I turn a long YouTube video into Shorts?

Paste the video link into SocialShot and press Make clips. It transcribes the video, scores every moment, and cuts the best ones into vertical Shorts with captions. A 15-minute video usually takes about two minutes.

02Is this YouTube Shorts maker free?

Every video gives you 3 clips for free, with no card needed. When a video deserves more, 1 credit makes 1 more clip. There is no subscription.

03Can it post to my YouTube channel?

Yes. Connect your YouTube account in SocialShot, then post a clip now or put it on the schedule.

04What kind of videos make the best Shorts?

Anything with people talking and reacting: streams, podcasts, interviews, commentary and reaction videos.
