01What is a live stream clipper?

A tool that makes clips from a stream while it is still live, instead of waiting for the full recording. SocialShot records in a short rolling buffer and saves clips of the moments people react to.

02Which platforms can it clip live?

Twitch, Kick and YouTube Live. Add a channel once and it is recorded each time it goes live.

03Does it keep the whole stream?

No. Live recordings are held only in a short rolling buffer while recording and then deleted. The clips are what you keep.

04Can I use it to clip for other streamers?

Yes, if you have their permission. Many clippers work in paid clipping programs that list the streams you may clip; follow each creator's rules.
