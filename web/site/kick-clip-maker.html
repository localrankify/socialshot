Common questions

Not sure it fits your stream? Paste one link and see what gets picked.

01Does SocialShot work with Kick?

Yes. Add a channel as kick.com/name to record it whenever it goes live, or paste a link to a past Kick stream to clip that one.

02How long are the clips?

Most clips land between 20 and 60 seconds, which suits Shorts, Reels and TikTok. You can trim any clip in the editor.

03Do I need to download the Kick VOD first?

No. SocialShot fetches the video from the link. You can also upload a recording from your computer.

04Can I clip IRL streams?

Yes. IRL streams work well because so much of the action is in what people say and how chat reacts, which is what SocialShot listens for.
