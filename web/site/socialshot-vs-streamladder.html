01Is StreamLadder free?

StreamLadder has a free plan for basic editing of Twitch clips into vertical videos. Reviews disagree on which AI features and export settings are free, so check streamladder.com for the current plans.

02What is the main difference?

StreamLadder is best known for formatting Twitch clips you already have into vertical layouts. SocialShot finds the moments for you, from live streams or VOD links, and returns them already vertical and captioned.

03Does SocialShot work with Kick?

Yes. SocialShot clips Kick streams live and from past-stream links, alongside Twitch and YouTube.
