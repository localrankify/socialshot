01Does Vizard have a free plan?

Yes. Reviews describe a free plan with a monthly credit allowance, 720p exports and a watermark. Paid plans remove the watermark. Check vizard.ai/pricing for current limits.

02What is a good Vizard alternative for streamers?

If you clip live streams, SocialShot records Twitch, Kick and YouTube channels as they go live and clips the best moments before the stream ends, with 3 free clips on every video.

03Is SocialShot a full video editor?

SocialShot is a clipping tool with a built-in clip editor: trim, captions, styles and layouts. For long-form editing you would still use a dedicated editor.
