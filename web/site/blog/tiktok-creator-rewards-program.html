TikTok Creator Rewards Program: requirements, pay and how clips fit in

TikTok only pays for original videos longer than a minute. Here are the requirements, what creators report earning, and how to cut streams into clips that qualify.

SocialShot team17 Nov 20262 min read

"TikTok Creator Rewards Program" is one of the most searched creator terms of 2026. TikTok's main payment program pays for views, but only on videos that meet a few rules, and the most important one surprises people coming from Shorts: videos have to be longer than one minute. Here is what you need, what it pays, and how stream clips fit in.

Creator Rewards requirements

Guides published in 2026 agree on these requirements. TikTok changes them and where the program runs, so check Creator tools in the app for the rules in your country.

  • 18 or older (19 in South Korea)
  • 10,000 followers
  • 100,000 video views in the last 30 days
  • An account in good standing
  • Living in a country where the program is available

You need to keep meeting them, not just meet them once when you apply.

Which videos earn

  • Longer than one minute. A 45-second clip can still grow your account, but it does not earn from the program.
  • Original. Your own footage and edits. Reposts, duets and stitches of other people's videos generally do not count.
  • Qualified views. Views are filtered. Very short watches and views that look fake are left out.

What it pays

TikTok does not publish a rate per view. Creators commonly report earning around $0.40 to $1.00 per 1,000 qualified views, with wide differences by country, watch time and how people engage with the video. A video that people watch to the end and search for tends to earn more than one that is scrolled past.

Creator Rewards is one way to earn on TikTok, not the only one. LIVE gifts, subscriptions, the TikTok Shop and brand deals each have their own rules. For clippers, paid clipping campaigns are often a better fit.

Turning stream clips into videos that qualify

Most stream clips are 20 to 40 seconds: one moment, one punchline. To earn from Creator Rewards you need more than a minute that still holds attention. That works best when the clip is a short story, not one moment:

  1. Set up the moment. Give 10 to 15 seconds of what led to it, so the payoff lands.
  2. Keep the payoff and the reaction. Chat going wild, the streamer's second reaction, the replay.
  3. Cut the dead air. Every pause you remove keeps people watching past the one-minute mark.
  4. Caption every word. Many people watch without sound. Word-by-word auto captions keep them reading.
  5. Make a short cut too. Post a sub-minute version on YouTube Shorts and Reels, where shorter often works better.

A live stream clipper saves the moments as they happen. You can then lengthen the ones with a story into one-minute TikToks and keep the quick ones short for Shorts.

Questions

01What are the TikTok Creator Rewards Program requirements?

Guides in 2026 agree on: at least 18 years old (19 in South Korea), 10,000 followers, 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, an account in good standing, and living in a country where the program runs. Check Creator tools in the app for the current rules where you live.

02How long do TikTok videos need to be for Creator Rewards?

Videos must be original and longer than one minute to earn. Shorter videos still help you grow, but they do not earn from the program.

03How much does TikTok pay per 1,000 views?

TikTok does not publish a rate. Creators commonly report around $0.40 to $1.00 per 1,000 qualified views, and it varies with location, watch time and engagement.

04Can I post stream clips for Creator Rewards?

Yes, if they are your own streams or you have permission, they are edited and they run longer than a minute. Unedited reposts of other people's content are not original and do not qualify.
