The best time to post YouTube Shorts, and how to find yours

Big 2026 studies point to Friday evening, but others say mornings. Here is what the data shows, why it disagrees, and a two-week test that finds your own best time.

SocialShot team17 Nov 20262 min read

Search "best time to post YouTube Shorts" and you will get a different answer from every site. The honest answer has two parts: what the big studies found in 2026, and the test that finds the right time for your viewers. The second matters more.

What the 2026 studies found

StudySizeBest time for Shorts
Buffer (2026)About 1.8 million videosFriday, with 4 pm, 6 pm and 7 pm the top hours
SocialPilot (2026)301,000+ videosFriday 4 to 7 pm; then Saturday and Thursday
PostFast (2026)6,175 postsMornings

The two largest studies agree on Friday late afternoon and early evening, in the viewer's local time. A smaller data set disagrees and points to mornings.

Why the studies disagree

  • They measure different things. Buffer measured engagement, not views or watch time.
  • Their accounts are different. A study full of brands behaves differently from one full of gaming clippers.
  • Time zones. "6 pm" is only useful if most of your viewers live in one time zone. A channel split between India and the US has two evenings.
  • Shorts last longer than a post. A Short can be shown for days after posting, so the first hour matters less than on other platforms.
Timing changes how many people see your Short in its first hours. The hook and how long people keep watching decide what happens after that. A great Short posted at a bad time still finds its audience; a weak one posted at the perfect time does not.

A two-week test to find your best time

  1. Check your audience. In YouTube Studio go to Analytics, then Audience, then When your viewers are on YouTube. Note the darkest hours.
  2. Pick three slots. One just before your busiest hour, one in the middle of the day, and one from the studies (Friday evening).
  3. Rotate for two weeks. Post one Short a day, moving between the slots. Use clips of similar quality so the test is fair.
  4. Compare after 48 hours. For each Short, note views and average percentage viewed two days after posting.
  5. Keep the winner and test again every few months; audiences change.

Questions

01What is the best time to post YouTube Shorts?

Two large 2026 studies, from Buffer and SocialPilot, found Friday between about 4 pm and 7 pm in the viewer's local time strongest for Shorts. Other data points to mornings. Your own audience's active hours in YouTube Studio matter more than any study.

02Does posting time matter for Shorts?

Less than for long videos. Shorts can keep getting shown for days, so a strong hook and good retention matter much more than the hour you post. Timing mostly helps the first test audience see it.

03How many Shorts should I post a day?

Post as often as you can keep quality up. One good Short a day beats five weak ones, because weak Shorts can teach the feed that your channel is skipped.

04Where do I see when my viewers are online?

In YouTube Studio, open Analytics, then Audience, and look at When your viewers are on YouTube. It needs some history before it shows.
