How to clip on Kick, and make Kick clips that travel

Where the clip button is, the shortcut, what the length limit is, and how to get Kick clips onto TikTok, Shorts and Reels.

SocialShot team17 Nov 20262 min read

Kick has become home to some of the biggest IRL and gaming streams, and their best moments spread through clips. Here is how to clip on Kick yourself, and how to turn those clips into vertical videos that work on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

How to clip on Kick on desktop

  1. Open the live stream on kick.com.
  2. Click the clip icon in the player controls, or press C.
  3. Use the slider to choose where the clip starts and ends.
  4. Add a title and publish. Your clip appears on the channel's clips page with a link to share.

How to clip on Kick on mobile

In the Kick app, tap the player to bring up the controls and choose Clip. Trim it, add a title and publish.

How long can a Kick clip be?

This is where guides disagree. Older guides say Kick clips can be up to 60 seconds. More recent guides quote a Kick Help Center article that allows up to 180 seconds. Because the limit has changed before, check Kick's Help Center for the current number. For TikTok, Shorts and Reels you will usually want 20 to 60 seconds anyway.

Clipping someone else's stream to repost it needs their permission. Many Kick streamers run clipping programs that say what you may clip and how to credit them.

Turning Kick clips into TikToks and Shorts

A native Kick clip is horizontal with no captions. On vertical feeds it shows up small and silent. Before you post, a Kick clip should be:

  • Vertical, cropped to the streamer or the action.
  • Captioned word by word, so it works with the sound off. See auto captions.
  • Trimmed to start just before the moment and end on the reaction.

What makes a Kick clip spread

  • The reaction is the content. On IRL streams, the faces and the chat matter as much as what happened.
  • Context in one line. Put a short hook on screen so a new viewer understands the moment instantly.
  • Speed. Post the same day, before someone else does.
