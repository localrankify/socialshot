01Is SocialShot free?

Every video gives you 3 clips for free, with no card needed. When you want more clips from a video, 1 credit makes 1 more clip. There is no subscription.

02What is the main difference from Opus Clip?

SocialShot is built around live streams: it records Twitch, Kick and YouTube channels as they go live and clips them before the stream ends. It also works on uploaded videos and links. Check each tool's own site for its current features and prices, which change often.

03Can I move my workflow over?

Yes. Paste the same video links you use today, or add your channels so new streams are clipped automatically.
